<p> My name is Matias, and I am a second-year student in the <a href="https://math.unc.edu/">Department of Mathematics</a> at the <a href="https://www.unc.edu/">University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill</a>. I am currently advised by Professor <a href = "https://sites.google.com/site/oliviamireladumitrescu/home?authuser=0">Olivia Dumitrescu</a>, working in birational geometry.</p>

<p> I was a Visiting Research Fellow at the <a href = "https://mathematics.huji.ac.il/">Einstein Institute of Mathematics</a> REU at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem for Summer 2026, advised by Professor <a href = "https://mathematics.huji.ac.il/people/shai-evra">Shai Evra</a> (read about it <a href = "https://matiascyrelyea.github.io/2026/einsteinreu/">here</a>). </p>

<p> This website serves the dual purpose of presenting both expository and original work pertaining to areas that I am interested in (under <a href = "https://matiascyrelyea.github.io/articles/">Articles</a>), as well as acting as a semi-serious pseudo-blog (under <a href = "https://matiascyrelyea.github.io/memoirs/">Memoirs</a>) that documents some of my mathematical experiences, opinions, and sometimes existential pieces that make no sense. </p>

<p> Currently I am interested in algebraic number theory, algebraic geometry, algebraic topology, Lie theory, and group theory. I am also interested in thinking critically about the impact that artificial intelligence has on mathematical research and the general mathematics community; read the <a href = "https://leidendeclaration.ai/">Leiden Declaration</a>. Thank you for visiting this site. </p>
